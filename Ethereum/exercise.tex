The tic-tac-toe game is a traditional game where two players alternate
playing on a $3\times 3$ board. One player fills the board's elements with
a cross tile, and plays first; the other player fills the board's elements with
a circle tile. A player wins as soon as he
fills three board's elements with
his own tile, either orizontally, or vertically or diagonally.
If the nine boards elements get full with tiles, but nobody wins, the game
is a draw.

Complete the following, incomplete Solidity smart contract, where marked with a
\textbf{TODO}. This contract requires the cross player to send some money
to the contract to start a new game and the circle player to send
at least the same money to accept the game. After the first two moves,
the next moves do not require payments, but these still remain possible.
If a player wins, he earns $80\%$ of the total money sent for the game;
the opponent earns $5\%$ of the total money; the deployer (\emph{owner}) of
the contract earns the remaining $15\%$ of the total money. In case of a draw,
all money collected for the game goes to the owner of the contract.
For simplicity, payments are immediate: do not implement the withdrawal pattern.

\textbf{Download the incomplete contract from \url{https://github.com/spoto/blockchain-course/blob/master/Ethereum/contracts/TicTacToeDraft.sol}}
